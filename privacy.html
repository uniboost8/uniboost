Privacy and cancellation

What Uni Boost keeps when you book a class, and how to cancel.

What we collect

A booking stores your name, phone number, email if you add one, university, the subject, and a 6 to 10 digit access code. If you pay by InstaPay, we also store the transfer reference and the screenshot you upload.

We use those details to hold your seat, confirm the payment, and show you the class link, materials, and recording in My Classes. We do not sell them.

Access code

The access code stays in this browser only until you close the tab. Your phone number can stay on the device so the form is easier to fill next time.

Class times

Every class time is Egypt time (Africa/Cairo), including for students opening the site from another country.

Cancellation

You can cancel an upcoming class from My Classes before it starts. A payment marked pending has not been confirmed yet. After Uni Boost confirms a payment, a refund is only arranged if you cancel before the class starts and we agree to return it.
